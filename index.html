Sources & about

Starts in light. Your choice is saved in this browser. System follows your browser or device.

2025 ICF Core Competencies, released September 2025, revision 09.08.25, PDF pp. 3-10. Each question, conversation review and exam example names its page and official sub-competency numbers so you can check the source.

The titles and definitions marked "Official definition" are quoted from ICF. You can read the definitions after answering a question or finishing a rehearsal. Summaries, questions, explanations and the fictional conversation were written for this app.

C1 to C8 identify the core competencies. Type only the title in practice and exam rehearsal; the C number is a label.

Correct titles you remember without help count as "Without hints". Using a hint or choosing Pass counts as "With hints", even if you leave without answering. Correct question answers are counted separately because you chose from options. These counts don't measure PCC competence or tell you whether you're ready for certification. This is not an official ICF assessment or an ICF-endorsed app. The client and replies are invented; a real conversation could go differently, and a reply alone doesn't prove that you've met a marker.

Your saved work stays in this browser. It includes practice counts, results and review dates. Exam rehearsal also saves your titles, statements, start time and optional deadline so you can return to them. The app doesn't save what you type in title practice or which question option you chose. There is no login, AI, tracking or outside service needed to run it. Nothing syncs between devices. Private browsing or clearing site data can erase your progress. Use invented examples in rehearsal and save a text copy for a backup.

When to practice again: a wrong answer, hint or Pass means trying again in 10 minutes and starting the review schedule over. Correct titles without hints and correct question answers move to 1, 3, then 7 days between reviews if you keep getting them right. New items are ready now. The intervals are this app's study schedule.
